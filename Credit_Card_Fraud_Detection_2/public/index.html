How it works

  1. Score: Random Forest estimates fraud probability from amount, category, hour, and day of week.
  2. Decide: at or below 20% is labelled genuine; at or above 80% is labelled fraud; values in between go to review.
  3. Review: reviewer decisions are recorded in this browser's feedback log. They do not automatically retrain the model.

Dataset and limitations

Kartik2112's Kaggle dataset is simulated transaction data. The selected fields are readable, but predictions depend only on these selected features. Do not treat results as actual bank decisions.

Model performance will be shown after training metrics are generated. Do not reuse the old ULB model's scores.
